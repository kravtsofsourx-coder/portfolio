Redesign of a gadgets and electronics online store

Goals

  • Increase customer loyalty to the brand.
  • Implement a new brand identity and communication strategy.
  • Improve conversion rate as well as AOV and LTV metrics.

Data analysis

Insight

Only 3.5% of users add a product to the cart.

Solution

Increased brand trust through a new visual style, added key information to support purchase decisions, and expanded service offerings.

Insight

50% of users abandon checkout.

Solution

Removed exit points from the checkout — specifically the header and footer.

Insight

70% of users visit from mobile, but engagement rates are low.

Solution

Focused on a well-designed, comfortable mobile experience.

Insight

Most users are new. A large growth opportunity.

Solution

Added more entry points on the home page: discounted, popular, and recommended products. On the product page: bonuses, reviews, and services.

Mechanics for increasing LTV

Benchmark analysis, feature list

Brand implementation. Key objectives:

  • Carry the brand's tone of voice and core message throughout the entire site: with Tehko — it's easy.
  • Implement all brand identity elements across the site pages.

Home page

  1. Open search as a focal point, reinforced with the right copywriting.
  2. "Buy easily" — the main banner CTA, based on the new tone of voice.
  3. Additional shortcuts for quick access to popular categories.

Product page

  1. A concise list of key specs for quick purchase decisions.
  2. Cross-sell services placed close to the "Buy" button.
  3. If the user hesitates on price, they have additional options: buy in instalments or use Trade-in.

Cart

  1. One-click purchase for users who need a quick checkout.
  2. Ability to share the cart when a joint purchase decision is needed.
  3. Cross-sell in the cart as well.

Checkout

  1. Only the logo and phone number are kept in the header to minimise distractions during checkout.
  2. Ability to view pickup locations on a map.
  3. Signed-in users can select a delivery address from their saved addresses.

Account

  1. Users can rate the quality of service, helping to respond to customer feedback in a timely manner.
  2. Users can also submit a repair request or initiate a return directly on the site.
