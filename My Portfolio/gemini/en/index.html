Redesign of a B2B portal and online store

Goals

  • Position the Gemini brand as Business To All.
  • Convince customers that this is where they'll find the best coffee.
  • Create a smooth ordering process that answers all questions for both new and experienced users upfront.
  • Demonstrate the company's high level of expertise.
  • Incorporate the new brand identity into the redesign.

Data analysis

Insight 1

73.1% of users visit from mobile devices.

Solution

Focused on a well-designed, comfortable mobile experience.

Insight 2

85% are new users. A large growth opportunity.

Solution

Added a USP section and detailed all information related to the ordering process, coffee, and coffee machines.

The fairly short session duration and high exit rate further confirm that users lack enough information to make a decision.

Discovery

I conducted interviews with the company's customer-facing managers and identified the key insights:

Insight 1

Customers often complain that their order arrived later than expected, even though this rarely happens when orders are placed in advance.

Solution

Displayed the estimated delivery date directly in the repeat order block.

Insight 2

Many users call in to ask about coffee machine specs or the rental process.

Solution

Surfaced all necessary information directly on the site to reduce the load on the call centre.

Prototyping

Worked through the main flow and addressed all goals and insights.

Home page

  1. Repeat order functionality for returning customers.
  2. Delivery date highlighted so users can plan ahead.
  3. A short company video for new users.
  4. USP block on the second screen for new users, with carefully crafted microcopy.
  1. The company covers a full range of services. These are listed further down the page to demonstrate expertise.

Coffee machine rental

  1. Shows which type of business a given coffee machine is best suited for.
  2. Users can choose the quantity and type of coffee and immediately see the price.
  1. Detailed specs, a description, the rental process, and reviews — all in one place to answer key questions and support the decision.

Equipment in instalments

Instalments are offered per package. Preliminary instalment prices, terms, and additional information are all clearly laid out.

The form for a more precise calculation is split into three steps to reduce cognitive load.

Account

  1. The account also includes a repeat last order option — wholesale clients typically order roughly the same volume of coffee each month.
  2. Key information about rentals and instalment plans is displayed prominently so users can quickly see how much coffee has been ordered and how much remains under the contract.
